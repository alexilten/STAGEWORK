## Engine features added for the library

| Feature | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Round and cross centre stages | `engine/deck.ts` | `shape: 'round'` is a custom circular deck (diameter = width) on hire substructure; `cross` is a centre square with four arms of hire platforms. Runways can end in a round B-stage. |
| Arc / ring LED | `engine/led.ts` | `radius` bends a wall to a true radius (concave or convex) and caps a ring at 360°. `yawDeg` turns a whole wall. `base: 'floor'` measures elevation from the venue floor. |
| LED floors and ceilings | `engine/led.ts` | `surface: 'floor'` lays tiles face up and needs a floor-rated product (the ROE Black Marble BM4 is in the catalogue); `surface: 'ceiling'` flies them face down. |
| Standing audience (GA) | `engine/audience.ts` | Front or surround floor. Capacity = clear area × density (2 / m² planning, 4 / m² treated as the safe maximum). Figures are drawn lighter (≤ 0.8 / m²) so the floor stays readable. Barricade follows the deck edge at the pit depth. |
| Raked stands | `engine/audience.ts` | Straight or arc stands with stepped treads and chairs in blocks with aisles. Every row is checked for C-value (60 mm minimum, 90 recommended, 120 optimum). |
| Ground-support roofs | `engine/structure.ts` | Stageco Roof 25 × 10, Classic 4-Tower and Super Roof envelopes from Stageco's published figures, with PA wings and wing IMAG. |
| Mother grid, delays, free truss | `engine/structure.ts` | A truss grid over the stage, delay towers with hangs and a delay estimate at 343 m/s, and free truss members between any two points (built from real section lengths). |
| Four-way PA | `engine/audio.ts` | `layout: 'quad'` hangs on the four diagonals of a centre stage; out-fill hangs for end stages. |
| DJ booth, scenic blocks, LED dome | `engine/show.ts` | CDJ-3000 footprints and a generic mixer on a booth table; scenic façades / plinths; a hemispherical dome of generic tiles with an optional opening. |
